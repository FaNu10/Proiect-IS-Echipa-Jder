About LittleLoop

LittleLoop is a marketplace built for families — a place to buy, sell, and donate the children's items your little ones have outgrown.

Kids grow fast. Clothes, toys, and gear are often barely used before they're too small. Instead of letting them collect dust, LittleLoop helps you pass them on to another family who will love them just as much.

Sell items your child has outgrown and earn a little back. Donate items for free and give them a second life with a family that needs them.

Every listing helps reduce waste, saves money for families, and keeps quality children's products in circulation — rather than in landfill.

Join our growing community of parents making smarter, kinder choices for their kids and the planet.
